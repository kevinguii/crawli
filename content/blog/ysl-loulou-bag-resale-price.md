## Why the same bag costs so much more on one site

- **Size tiers are genuinely different products.** The toy, small, and medium Loulou share the same design but price very differently even in identical condition.
- **The Loulou backpack variant** exists within the same line and prices independently of the standard shoulder bag silhouette.
- **Leather finish and hardware color** vary in desirability, creating price gaps even between two bags in equally good condition.

## How to vet a cheap Loulou listing

- **Y-shaped quilting.** Genuine stitching is even and consistent across the entire bag in Saint Laurent's signature pattern, distinct from Chanel's diamond or Gucci's chevron quilting.
- **Cassandre logo hardware.** Should be substantial with crisp, precise stamping; light or slightly blurred hardware is a common tell.
- **Leather hand-feel.** Genuine lambskin is soft and supple; a stiff or plasticky feel is a warning sign.
- **Interior lining and stitching,** which should match the specific size and be tight and even throughout.
- **Request close-up photos of the quilting and hardware stamp** before paying on any listing priced well under the rest of the market.
