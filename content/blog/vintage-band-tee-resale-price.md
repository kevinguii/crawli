**Short answer:** As of July 2026, the same style of vintage band tee lists from about $4 on Vinted to roughly $30 on Grailed, with Depop, Poshmark, and Mercari sitting between at $7 to $25. The cheapest listings are usually cheap because the seller didn't recognize what they had, not because the tee itself is less valuable, which is exactly why cross-marketplace search and a quick authenticity check pay off here.

## Why the same tee costs more on one site

- **Seller recognition, not tee quality.** A Vinted or Depop seller clearing a closet often doesn't know they're holding a rare 1980s tour tee versus a common reprint, and prices both the same.
- **Buyer intent by platform.** Mercari and Grailed attract more buyers specifically searching for band merchandise, which pulls both the floor and the average higher.
- **Rarity within the category.** A specific, documented tour print sells for far more than a generic long-running band logo, even within the same platform.

## The one check that matters most before buying

Before paying for a cheap band tee, flip the hem inside out. A single row of stitching points toward genuine pre-mid-1990s construction; two parallel rows point toward a later reprint. This is the fastest of five checks we cover in full in [How to Tell a 1970s Vintage Band Tee From a 1990s Reprint](/blog/how-to-identify-vintage-band-tee), which also covers tag makers (Screen Stars, Brockum, Winterland), fabric weight, print texture, and fade consistency. Run that check before assuming a $4 to $7 listing is a deal.
