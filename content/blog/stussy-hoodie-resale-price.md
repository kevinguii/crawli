## Why the same hoodie costs more on one site

- **Collaboration premiums on StockX and Vestiaire.** Stussy's collaborations with major brands trade at authenticated marketplace prices well above basic logo prints.
- **Peer-to-peer sellers pricing common prints low.** Vinted and Depop sellers offloading a standard World Tour or 8-Ball hoodie price it like any ordinary hoodie.
- **Curated marketplace expectations.** Grailed and Vestiaire buyers pay a premium for vetted listings even on non-collaboration pieces.

## How to vet a cheap Stussy hoodie listing

- **Interior neck tag.** Confirm correct Stussy branding and RN number formatting.
- **Screen-printed logo.** Should have clean edges, no cracking or bleeding.
- **Drawstring hardware and fabric weight.** Compare against known genuine examples.
- **Collaboration branding.** If the listing claims a specific collab, check it against verified reference images for that exact release before paying.
- **Ask which specific print or season** if the title is vague, since that detail drives most of the value difference here.
