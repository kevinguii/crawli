## Why the same bag costs so much more on one site

- **Oblique canvas versus smooth leather is the biggest price driver.** The printed monogram canvas version reads as more directly tied to Dior's current design language and commands a premium over plain leather in most sizes.
- **Size tiers price very differently.** The mini saddle, medium, and large sizes are distinct products even in identical material and condition.
- **Curated platforms weight documentation and box completeness more heavily,** which is part of why Vestiaire and ThredUp's averages sit well above Mercari or Depop's typical listing.

## How to vet a cheap Saddle Bag listing

- **Oblique canvas print alignment.** Should register cleanly across every seam, especially where the flap meets the body; misaligned printing is a common fake tell.
- **CD hardware clasp.** Genuine hardware is substantial with crisp, deep stamping; light or blurry stamping is a warning sign.
- **Interior lining and stitching.** Should match the specific bag's documented material, and stitching should be tight and even throughout.
- **Smell test.** A strong plastic or glue odor on either the canvas or leather version is a red flag.
- **Request close-up photos of the hardware stamp and interior tag** before paying on any listing priced well under the rest of the market.
