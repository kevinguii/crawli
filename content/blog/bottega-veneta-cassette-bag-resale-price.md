## Why the same bag costs so much more on one site

- **Size is the single biggest price driver.** The mini pouch-style Cassette and the full crossbody version share a name but are different products with different demand.
- **Intrecciato weave color and width vary in desirability.** Certain shades and the wider "Candy" weave pattern (as seen in some collaboration pieces) command a premium over the standard narrow weave.
- **Bottega Veneta's minimal branding makes condition and weave quality do more of the selling work** than a logo would on a more heavily branded bag, which is part of why curated platforms with detailed condition photos command higher prices.

## How to vet a cheap Cassette listing

- **Intrecciato weave consistency.** Should be tight, even, and uniform in strip width across every panel, including the back and gusset.
- **Internal tag embossing.** Should be crisp and evenly spaced; shallow or uneven embossing is a common tell.
- **Padded leather substance,** on the Padded Cassette variant, which should feel structured and substantial rather than thin or hollow.
- **External branding.** Bottega Veneta is known for minimal external logos; unusually prominent branded hardware on a piece claiming to be a standard Cassette is worth extra scrutiny.
- **Request close-up photos of the weave and internal tag** before paying on any listing priced well under the rest of the market.
