## Why the same bag costs so much more on one site

- **Size and strap configuration are different products.** A Speedy 25, Speedy 30, and Speedy Bandouliere (with the added crossbody strap) are all called "Speedy" but occupy different price tiers even in identical condition.
- **The Speedy is one of the most counterfeited Louis Vuitton bags,** which pushes real buyers toward authenticated platforms for anything beyond a casual peer-to-peer purchase, widening the gap between unverified and verified listings.
- **Vachetta leather condition and handle wear** matter more on the Speedy than most LV bags, since the handles see constant contact and develop patina (or damage) faster than the body of the bag.

## How to vet a cheap Speedy listing

- **Heat-stamped date code.** Check the format and factory location against known Louis Vuitton reference charts; a mismatch with the claimed era is disqualifying.
- **Handle vachetta leather.** Should be raw tan when new, developing natural patina over years, not artificially pre-darkened.
- **Zipper pull and engraving.** Genuine zipper pulls are substantial with clean, deep engraving; light or shallow engraving is a common tell.
- **Stitching at the handle attachment points,** which take the most stress and are a common weak point on convincing fakes.
- **Confirm the size and strap configuration** before comparing a listing's price against others, since Speedy 25, 30, and Bandouliere are priced as different products.

## Turning a well-sourced Speedy into margin

Source a verified-authentic bag near the Depop or Grailed floor in a common size, and resell toward the Vestiaire or Poshmark range.
