Read the listing description carefully before assuming a low number represents the full tote: the St Louis pouch is a genuine, separately sold accessory that shares search terms with the tote, and a meaningful share of the cheapest listings on Depop and Mercari are the pouch alone.

## Why the same name covers such a wide range

- **The pouch and the tote are different products sold under overlapping names.** Both are genuine Goyard items, but comparing a pouch's price to a tote's price isn't a real comparison.
- **PM and GM sizes price differently** even within confirmed full-size tote listings.
- **Hand-painted personalization adds real value** for some buyers and is irrelevant or even a negative for others who want a blank canvas, which creates price variation independent of size or condition.

## How to vet a cheap St Louis listing

- **Confirm the item first.** Read the full description and check photos for the actual tote dimensions before comparing price to the ranges above.
- **Goyardine canvas texture.** Genuine canvas is printed, not embossed, with a specific flat, slightly textured feel; embossed or overly glossy canvas is a common fake tell.
- **Hand-painted personalization,** where present, has a distinctive brushed texture from an in-house artisan; anything claiming boutique personalization done elsewhere is worth extra scrutiny.
- **Leather trim and handle stitching,** which should be substantial and evenly done throughout.
- **Request close-up photos of the canvas texture and leather trim** before paying on any listing priced well under the confirmed-tote range.
