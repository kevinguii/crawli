## Why the same bag costs so much more on one site

- **Leather type is the single biggest price driver.** The standard chevron-quilted leather Marmont and an exotic-skin version share a silhouette but occupy entirely different price tiers.
- **Size and form factor vary widely under one name.** Mini, small, medium, the larger tote, and the backpack version all get listed as "Marmont" but appeal to different buyers at different price points.
- **Since 2021, genuine bags include an NFC microchip** for in-app authentication, and buyers increasingly factor that verification option into what they're willing to pay on unauthenticated peer-to-peer platforms.

## How to vet a cheap Marmont listing

- **Interlocking GG hardware weight and alignment.** Should be substantial and precisely centered; light or slightly off-center hardware is a common tell.
- **NFC microchip (2021 and later bags).** Ask the seller to confirm the bag scans correctly in the Gucci app, which is a strong authenticity signal a counterfeit cannot replicate.
- **Matelasse quilting consistency.** The diamond pattern should be even in size across every panel, including the back and gusset.
- **Chevron pattern alignment at the seams,** which counterfeiters frequently get wrong where panels meet.
- **Request close-up photos of the hardware stamp and interior tag** before paying on any listing priced well under the rest of the market.
