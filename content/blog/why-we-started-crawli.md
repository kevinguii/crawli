# Why I Started Crawli

I still remember back when I was a sophomore in college, looking for ways to spice up my wardrobe. I had seen friends with shirts I wanted, styles I wanted to dress as, and shoes that I thought would look good on me. But I was balling on a budget. That's when I started to fall deep into the rabbit hole of second-hand fashion.

There were an endless array of stores to look from and an endless array of options to look at. But I never found the grail I was looking for—something stylish that no one else had. I could either never find it, or it was always sold out, or it was always too expensive for me. I ended up buying a "vintage" jacket that was a little too distressed for my liking. $20 and hours of research looking for the grail, just for me to throw it away as soon as it arrived and request a refund. The search process was tireless, and I never found what I really wanted.

That's why I decided to build Crawli.

Crawli is my first passion project. I wanted to tailor this product to two audiences: people like sophomore me just looking for a good deal, and resellers who are looking to do research on things they want to sell. I hope this product can deliver on the needs of the overall second-hand community, and I hope to provide what is needed.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out at hello@thecrawli.com.

Happy Crawling.
