## Why the same shoe costs this much more on one site

- **Common vs collaboration colorways in the same search.** The Dunk Low spans everyday releases and heavily hyped collaborations, and averages swing hard depending on which mix a platform's active listings happen to have.
- **Authentication built into StockX's floor.** Buyers pay for verification on every purchase, which raises the entry price even for a common colorway.
- **Peer-to-peer underpricing.** Casual sellers on Depop, Mercari, and Vinted often don't know what a rarer colorway is worth and price it like any other pair.

## How to vet a cheap Dunk Low listing

- **Read the listing carefully.** The lowest-priced listings occasionally include decoys like shoebox-only sales or heavily mismatched sizes. Confirm the photos show the actual shoes.
- **Swoosh stitching.** Clean, even edges on both shoes.
- **Toe box perforations.** Evenly spaced and consistent.
- **Tongue tag and size label.** Match against a known-authentic reference for the specific colorway.
- **Outsole tread and midsole color.** Should be consistent with the stated condition.
