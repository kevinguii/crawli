## Why the same "scarf" covers such a wide range

- **Size format is a bigger factor than most buyers expect.** The full 90cm carre, the narrower twilly, and the pocket-square pochette are all genuine Hermes silk pieces sold at very different price points.
- **Pattern rarity drives real demand differences.** A discontinued or especially collected print commands a premium over a currently-produced common pattern, independent of size or condition.
- **Condition of the hand-rolled hem matters more here than on most accessories,** since it's both a key authentication signal and a visible wear point that affects resale value directly.

## How to vet a cheap scarf listing

- **Hand-rolled hem.** Should be slightly irregular but tight and consistent under close inspection; a too-uniform machine-rolled edge or visible glue is a common fake tell.
- **Silk weight and sheen.** Genuine Hermes silk twill has a specific substantial weight and subtle sheen that's hard to replicate in cheaper silk.
- **Print registration and color separation.** Should be sharp and clean with no blurring, even in intricate multi-color designs.
- **Confirm the size format** (carre, twilly, or pochette) before comparing a listing's price to others.
- **Request close-up photos of the hem and a corner of the print** before paying on any listing priced well under the rest of the market.
