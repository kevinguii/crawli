## Why the same hoodie costs 6x more on one site

- **Counterfeit risk drives real premium.** The Shark Hoodie is one of the most faked pieces in streetwear, so StockX's authentication carries genuine value buyers are willing to pay for.
- **Camo pattern and season rarity.** Specific camo colorways and limited collaboration runs command steep premiums on Grailed and Vestiaire that a common-season hoodie doesn't.
- **Peer-to-peer sellers underpricing rarity.** A Depop or Mercari seller clearing a closet often doesn't know which season or camo they're holding, and prices it like any other hoodie.

## How to vet a cheap Shark Hoodie listing

- **Shark face zipper pull.** Genuine pulls have real heft and sharply defined detail; fakes often feel light and look soft-edged.
- **Camo registration and color separation.** Should be crisp with clean edges between colors, not blurry or bleeding.
- **Interior neck tag.** Match the tag design against a known-authentic reference for the specific season.
- **Hood shark teeth stitching.** Even, clean lines with no loose threads.
- **Request close-up photos** of the zipper pull, tag, and hood before paying, especially on listings under $60.
