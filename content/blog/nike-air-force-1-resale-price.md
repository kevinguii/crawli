## Why the same shoe costs 7x more on one site

- **Authentication premium.** StockX's floor includes verification. On a shoe this common, that added cost is a large share of the sale price relative to the shoe's actual scarcity.
- **Closet-clearing supply on Depop.** The Air Force 1's ubiquity means huge peer-to-peer supply, and sellers clearing space price accordingly low.
- **Colorway and collaboration mix.** Rare Off-White or Travis Scott-style collaborations pull the Grailed and Vestiaire averages up even when the everyday white-on-white colorway stays cheap everywhere.

## How to vet a cheap Air Force 1 listing

- **Swoosh stitching.** Should be clean and even on both sides, with no loose threads.
- **Toe box perforations.** Evenly spaced and uniform in size.
- **Tongue tag and size label.** Match font and format against a known-authentic reference for the release.
- **Sole texture and midsole color.** Should be consistent and match the shoe's stated age and condition.
- **Ask for extra photos on sub-$15 listings**, especially on rarer colorways.
