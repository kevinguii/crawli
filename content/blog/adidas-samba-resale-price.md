## Why the same shoe costs 4x more on one site

- **Trend-peak dispersion.** A shoe this in-demand attracts both closet-clearing casual sellers and collectors chasing specific colorways in the same marketplace, which spreads the price further than a shoe with steady, unremarkable demand.
- **Audience expectations.** Vestiaire and Poshmark buyers expect a curated, verified listing and pay accordingly. Depop and Mercari sellers are often just clearing space and price to move.
- **Colorway skew.** OG and collaboration colorways command real premiums on Grailed and Vestiaire, which pulls the average up even when the platform's floor for a common colorway stays low.

## How to vet a cheap Samba listing

- **Gum sole texture and color.** Fakes often get the gum tone slightly wrong or use a texture that's too smooth or too matte.
- **Stitching on the leather panels.** Look for even, tight stitching with no visible glue overflow at the seams.
- **Trefoil and stripe branding.** Genuine branding is cleanly applied with sharp edges, not blurred or slightly misaligned.
- **Box label and size tag.** Match the style code and tag font against a known-authentic reference for the specific colorway before paying.
- **Ask for more photos on the cheap end.** A $16 to $20 listing without close-ups deserves a message before you commit.
