## Why the same fleece costs more on one site

- **Curated marketplace premium.** Vestiaire buyers expect a vetted, verified listing and pay accordingly, even for a common current colorway.
- **Peer-to-peer volume pricing.** Grailed and Vinted sellers list common solids they no longer wear, pricing to move quickly given how widely available the Better Sweater is secondhand.
- **Pattern and color rarity.** Discontinued fleece patterns and older heathered colorways command more from collectors than a current-season solid black or navy.

## How to vet a cheap Better Sweater listing

- **Interior neck tag.** Confirm correct Patagonia branding, care label, and country of origin.
- **Zipper hardware.** Should be a genuine branded or YKK zipper that pulls smoothly.
- **Fleece texture.** Match the grid or sherpa pattern to the claimed style; Patagonia has used several fleece constructions across years.
- **Shoulder seam and pocket stitching.** Clean, consistent, and free of loose threads.
- **Ask about pilling and shell wear**, since condition affects resale value on this piece more than most.
