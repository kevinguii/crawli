Any Datejust priced dramatically below that range is not a real Rolex at that price, whatever the listing claims.

## Why the real spread is still this wide

- **Case material changes the price tier entirely.** Steel, two-tone (steel and gold), and full precious-metal Datejusts are different products with very different values even in the same reference family.
- **Box, papers, and service history matter enormously at this price point.** A full set with original documentation and a recent service record sells for meaningfully more than the same watch on its own, and curated platforms weight this heavily in their pricing.
- **Dial color and bezel style drive collector demand.** Certain discontinued dial colors and fluted or diamond bezels command premiums that have little to do with the watch's basic mechanical value.

## How to verify a Datejust before you pay

- **Case engravings.** Older references show the serial number between the lugs; newer models move it to the rehaut, the inner rim visible through the crystal. Cross-check against known production date ranges.
- **Cyclops lens magnification.** Should magnify the date roughly 2.5 times; a flat or barely magnifying lens is a common fake tell.
- **Sweeping second hand.** Genuine automatic movements produce a smooth, continuous sweep, never a visible tick.
- **Weight and balance.** A real Datejust feels substantial and precisely weighted; unusually light cases are a warning sign.
- **Get independent third-party authentication** before paying on any private listing, regardless of how convincing the photos and described details look. At this price point, that step is not optional.

## Buying to resell at this price point

Genuine Datejusts below the authenticated-platform average do surface, usually from private sellers who need a fast sale. But treat this category with the same caution as a Birkin or Chanel bag: one authentication mistake here costs more than most people's entire flip business. Budget for third-party authentication as a fixed cost on every purchase.
