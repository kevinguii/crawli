## Why the same jacket costs 6x more on one site

- **Collaboration premiums on StockX.** Carhartt WIP's limited drops with other brands trade at authenticated marketplace prices that dwarf everyday workwear jackets.
- **Peer-to-peer sellers pricing common jackets low.** Depop and Vinted sellers offloading a standard Detroit or Michigan jacket price it like ordinary workwear because, for most of them, it is.
- **Curated marketplace expectations.** Vestiaire buyers expect a vetted listing and pay a premium accordingly, even on non-collaboration pieces.

## How to vet a cheap Carhartt WIP jacket listing

- **Interior label.** Confirm correct WIP branding and RN number formatting.
- **Triple-stitched seams.** Should be clean, even, and consistent throughout.
- **Fabric weight.** Genuine duck canvas or corduroy has a specific weight and hand-feel; compare against known reference photos.
- **Collaboration branding.** If the listing claims a specific collab, check placement and stitching quality against verified images of that exact release.
- **Ask which specific style and season** if the listing title is vague. Common jackets and collaboration pieces look similar in thumbnail photos.
