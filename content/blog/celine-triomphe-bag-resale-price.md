Before comparing prices across platforms, check the specific silhouette in each listing. A compact leather crossbody and a large canvas tote both carry Triomphe hardware but sell in very different ranges.

## Why the same name covers such a wide range

- **Triomphe is a hardware line, not a single bag.** The interlocking C clasp appears on totes, crossbody bags, and travel pieces, all searchable under the same term but priced as different products.
- **Material varies as much as silhouette.** Coated canvas, smooth calfskin, and the more casual macadam-print leather all appear across the line at different price points.
- **Curated platforms weight documentation and rare colorways more heavily,** which is part of why Poshmark and Vestiaire's higher listings sit well above Mercari's typical range.

## How to vet a cheap Triomphe listing

- **Triomphe hardware clasp.** Should be substantial with precise, symmetrical stamping of the interlocking logo; light or uneven hardware is a common tell.
- **Leather grain and smell.** Genuine Celine leather has a distinct texture and should never have a strong plastic or glue odor.
- **Engraved leather plate,** where present, should show crisp, deep engraving rather than shallow or blurry lettering.
- **Strap attachment stitching,** which should be tight and even, since this is a common stress point counterfeiters rush.
- **Confirm the exact silhouette** (tote, crossbody, or shoulder bag) before comparing a listing's price against others.
