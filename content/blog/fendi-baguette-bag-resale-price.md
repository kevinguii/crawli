## Why the same bag costs so much more on one site

- **Size tiers are genuinely different products.** Nano, mini, and standard Baguettes share a silhouette but price very differently even in identical fabric and condition.
- **Fabric ranges from canvas to leather to embellished special editions,** each with its own demand curve independent of size.
- **Rare collaboration prints command a real premium** over the standard Zucca monogram canvas, and this is the single biggest factor separating a typical listing from an outlier high price.

## How to vet a cheap Baguette listing

- **FF logo hardware clasp.** Should be substantial with precise, symmetrical stamping; light or uneven hardware is a common tell.
- **Zucca canvas print registration,** where present, should align cleanly across every seam.
- **Leather grain and smell,** on leather versions, which should never carry a strong plastic or glue odor.
- **Strap and flap stitching,** which should be tight and even throughout.
- **Extra scrutiny on rare collaboration prints specifically,** since these are the most frequently counterfeited version of this bag; request close-up photos of the hardware and interior tag before paying.
