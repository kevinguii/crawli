Before you jump at the lowest number on any given day, check whether the listing includes the matching pochette insert or is just the pochette by itself. Both get listed under "Neverfull," but they are very different purchases.

## Why the same tote costs so much more on one site

- **Size and canvas pattern set the baseline.** PM, MM, and GM sizes price differently even in identical condition, and Damier Azur or Ebene canvas commands different demand than the standard monogram.
- **Vachetta leather condition is the biggest swing factor within a size.** The untreated leather trim develops a natural honey-brown patina over years of use, which some buyers specifically seek out and others consider a flaw. A bag with heavily worn, dirty, or water-stained vachetta sells for meaningfully less regardless of the canvas condition.
- **Rare or discontinued colorways pull the average far above the standard version.** Artist collaborations and limited runs create the widest gaps between a common bag's floor and a rare bag's ceiling within the same silhouette.

## How to vet a cheap Neverfull listing

- **Heat-stamped date code.** Located inside the bag, it encodes the factory and production date in a format that can be checked against known Louis Vuitton reference charts. A code that doesn't match the claimed era is a clear red flag.
- **Canvas pattern registration.** Genuine LV monogram print is crisp and consistently aligned at every seam, including where panels meet at the sides. Blurry or misaligned printing is common on fakes.
- **Vachetta leather texture.** Should be raw and slightly stiff when new, never pre-darkened or artificially textured to look aged.
- **Hardware and stitching.** Zipper pulls and D-rings should feel substantial, and stitching should be tight and even, using a matching thread color to the canvas.
- **Confirm whether the pochette is included** before comparing a listing's price to others, since a tote-plus-pochette bundle and a solo pochette get listed under overlapping search terms.

## Turning a well-sourced Neverfull into margin

Source a verified-authentic bag near the Depop or Vestiaire floor in a common size and canvas, and resell toward the Poshmark or authenticated-platform range if you land a rarer colorway or exceptional vachetta condition.
