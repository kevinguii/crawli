## Why the price range is this wide

- **Season and colorway rarity, not platform, drives most of the value.** A common current-season box logo and a discontinued rare colorway can both be labeled "Supreme Box Logo Hoodie" while being worth wildly different amounts.
- **Authentication premium on StockX.** The Box Logo is one of the most counterfeited pieces in streetwear, so verified authenticity carries real, justified value.
- **Collector demand on curated platforms.** Vestiaire and Grailed buyers are often specifically hunting rare seasons, which pulls those platforms' averages far above their floors.

## How to vet a Box Logo listing

- **Font weight and letter spacing.** Supreme's box logo has changed subtly across seasons; compare the exact letterforms to reference images for the claimed season.
- **Interior tag and care label.** Should match known-authentic formatting for that era.
- **Stitching around the box print.** Clean application with no visible glue or fraying.
- **Be skeptical of unusually low prices** on a listing claiming a rare season. If a supposedly rare box logo is priced like a common one, verify carefully before paying.
