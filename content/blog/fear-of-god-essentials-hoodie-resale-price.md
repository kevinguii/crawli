## Why the same hoodie costs more on one site

- **Season and colorway rarity.** Essentials has released many seasons with subtly different branding and fits; older or discontinued colorways command more on Grailed and StockX than current-season pieces on Depop.
- **Curated marketplace premiums.** Grailed and StockX buyers expect verified, well-photographed listings and pay accordingly, even for common colorways.
- **Peer-to-peer volume pricing.** Depop and Mercari sellers often list current-season pieces they no longer wear, pricing to move rather than to match collector demand.

## How to vet a cheap Essentials hoodie listing

- **Interior tag branding.** Confirm the exact Fear of God Essentials wordmark and reflective logo placement match the claimed season.
- **Drawstring hardware and fabric weight.** Genuine pieces use consistent hardware and a specific heavyweight fleece; compare against known references.
- **Fit and sleeve proportions.** Essentials' boxy silhouette has shifted across seasons; verify the cut matches what the listing claims.
- **Ask which season** if unclear. Season and colorway matter more to value here than almost any other detail.
