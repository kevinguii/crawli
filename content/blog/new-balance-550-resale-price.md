## Why the same shoe costs this much more on one site

- **Peer-to-peer vs authenticated.** Depop and Vinted sellers are typically clearing a closet and price to move fast. StockX's floor includes verification you pay for on every purchase.
- **Curated marketplace premiums.** Vestiaire and Poshmark buyers expect vetted listings and pay a premium for that trust, even on a shoe as common as the 550.
- **Colorway and condition mix.** The 550 spans everyday colorways and rarer collaborations in the same search, and platforms with more collector traffic (Grailed, Vestiaire) skew higher on average as a result.

## How to vet a cheap 550 listing

- **N logo stitching.** The side-panel N should be evenly stitched and symmetrical on both shoes.
- **Perforated toe box.** Holes should be evenly spaced and consistent in size.
- **Tongue tag and insole branding.** Match against a known-authentic reference for the specific colorway.
- **Midsole condition.** Yellowing or wear should be consistent with the rest of the shoe, not isolated or artificial-looking.
- **Ask for extra photos on sub-$20 listings.** A legitimate seller sends them without hesitation.
