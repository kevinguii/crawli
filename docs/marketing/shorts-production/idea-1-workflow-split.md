**Format:** Split-screen (or sequential hard cut) showing manual chaos vs. Crawli calm  
**Runtime:** 4-5 seconds  
**Audience hook:** This audience has *lived* the tab-hell pain (language-file §2, §3 — "miserable," "tired of searching")  
**Why it wins:** No voiceover needed. The mess IS the story.

## Shooting script for on-camera moments

**Setup:** Camera on you (phone tripod is fine), neutral background (blank wall, or a clean desk/room corner). Lighting: natural window light is best, avoid harsh shadows on your face.

**Take 1: The "overwhelmed" shot**
- Open: You're sitting normally, neutral expression.
- Cue: As the chaotic screen recording plays (which you'll add in Resolve later), put your head in your hand — elbow on the table/desk.
- Action: Shake your head slowly side-to-side, like "this is so annoying." Eyes down or looking at the imaginary screen. Keep it real, not overacted.
- Hold: 3–4 seconds. Let it breathe.
- Close: Look up slightly at the end, so there's a natural transition to the next beat.

**Take 2: The "satisfied" shot**
- Open: You're sitting in the same position, neutral.
- Cue: As the calm Crawli screen plays, look at the imaginary screen and nod.
- Action: Nod once, slow and deliberate. Then a small satisfied smile or exhale (maybe "yeah"). If it feels natural, you can do a hands-together gesture (like "got it") or just relax your shoulders.
- Hold: 2–3 seconds. Keep the satisfied expression.
- Close: Hold the smile through the fade.

**Timing:** Each take should be 3–4 seconds of actual footage. Shoot 2–3 takes of each so you have options in the edit.

## DaVinci Resolve assembly

1. **Import:** two screen recordings (before/after), your two on-camera clips
2. **Timeline:** Drag them in this order:
   - Before screen recording (0:00–0:50)
   - Your overwhelmed face (0:50–1:10)
   - After screen recording (1:10–1:60)
   - Your satisfied face (1:60–2:30)
3. **Text overlays:** Add text in the Fusion page or the text panel:
   - "Me checking comps before Crawli" — appears at 0:00, stays through 1:10
   - "Me now" — appears at 1:10, stays through 2:30
   - Position: top-left or center, keep it out of the bottom 20% and right 15%
4. **Audio:** Drag your sound bed under the entire timeline. Fade it in at 0:00, fade it out at 2:30.
5. **Color:** Ensure both the light and dark versions read clearly. Test by exporting a 3-second clip first.
6. **Export:** 1080×1350 (4:5 ratio for Shorts), 30fps, 60fps audio.

## Asset list

### Screen recordings you need to capture yourself:
- **Before screen:** Your desktop with 4–5 marketplace tabs open. Open them in this order: Poshmark, eBay, Vinted, Depop, Grailed. Click between them 3–4 times. Open calculator app. Scroll down in one tab. Make it look genuinely chaotic — this is the hook.
- **After screen:** Log into Crawli, do a real search (search for something you actually flip or like), show the multi-marketplace results, scroll to the flip calculator, show the margin numbers. Real data is best — no faked numbers.

### Sounds
- **Before bed (chaotic):** Pick ONE from these (search on YouTube Audio Library or Epidemic Sound free tier):
  - "Digital Chaos" or "Busy Office" (search term)
  - Or just record the ambient sound of yourself clicking rapidly
  - Alt: use a simple alert/notification sound (royalty-free from Freesound.org)
  
- **After sound (satisfying):** Pick ONE:
  - "Success Chime" or "Positive Bell" (YouTube Audio Library)
  - A simple "ding" sound (Freesound.org, search "notification")
  - Or silence — the contrast with the chaos is the effect

### Transitions
- No fancy transitions needed. Hard cut on the beat drop is the move. Resolve: select the clips, right-click "Dissolve," set duration to 0 frames (true hard cut).

## Why this works for Shorts

1. **No voiceover:** Shorts' algorithm favors natural sound (marketplace sounds, your actual typing sounds, the alert chime). Voiceovers underperform on Shorts vs. TikTok.
2. **Face + contrast:** You on camera proves authenticity. The chaos-to-calm contrast is visual. Together = credible.
3. **Profile alignment:** This audience already knows the pain (Reddit corpus). You're not explaining it — you're validating it and showing the payoff.
4. **Sound design:** Satisfying sound on the reveal gets shares (this is why "before/after" format is crushing it on Shorts).
