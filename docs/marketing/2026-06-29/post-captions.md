The most underpriced vintage on eBay is spelled wrong.

## Post 02 — Rise Measurement Standard
`topic_key: rise-measurement-fit`
`#listing-quality #reseller-tips`

**TikTok Hook (first line):**
The measurement causing most of your pants returns is not the waist. It is the rise.

**Caption:**
A 32 inch waist on a 1980s high-rise sits three inches higher than a modern mid-rise with the exact same tag number.

Your buyer buys based on the waist. The return happens because of the rise.

Front rise: lay the pants flat, measure from the top of the waistband down the front seam to the crotch point. Back rise: same from the back seam.

List both. Specify front and back.

One extra measurement, no more returns for fit.

#resellertips #vintagedenim #thrifting #sellingtips #secondhandfashion #pantsfit

## Post 03 — MA-1 Orange Lining History
`topic_key: flight-jacket-lining-history`
`#vintage-community #era-guide`

**TikTok Hook (first line):**
The orange lining in a vintage flight jacket was not put there for style.

**Caption:**
In 1958 the US Air Force needed a jacket that could signal a downed pilot from the air.

The MA-1 was designed reversible. Olive drab outside for flight operations. International Orange inside for rescue visibility after an ejection.

The same orange used on the Golden Gate Bridge.

After 1967 the USAF dropped the orange standard and switched to sage green. That date is your quick dating tool.

Orange lining = pre-1967. Sage lining = after.

Confirm with the Talon zipper and the MIL-J spec label inside the left chest. The contract year is printed on it.

Once you see listings differently, you start finding them where others don't look.

#vintagejacket #ma1jacket #flightjacket #vintagemilitary #vintageouterwear #eraidentification

## Post 04 — Fake Ray-Ban Wayfarer Check
`topic_key: fake-rayban-wayfarer-check`
`#authentication #vintage-sunglasses`

**TikTok Hook (first line):**
Real Wayfarers have a tiny RB etched on the left lens. Fakes almost never get the font right.

**Caption:**
Five things to check before you buy a vintage Wayfarer.

One: the RB lens etching. Pre-1999 = fine delicate sans-serif. Post-1999 = thicker rounded letters. If the font is wrong or missing, it is not real.

Two: hinge count. Seven barrels on a real pair. Fakes use five.

Three: inside the temple arm. Model number plus a BL country stamp on authentic pairs.

Four: nose pads. Real pads are molded and attached. Fakes use adhesive pads that peel.

Five: price. Under 30 dollars as "authentic" on any platform is not authentic.

#rayban #wayfarers #vintagesunglasses #authenticating #fakevreal #buyingvintage

## Post 05 — Shearling Authentication
`topic_key: shearling-authentication`
`#material-authentication #vintage-outerwear`

**TikTok Hook (first line):**
Most shearling coats listed online are not shearling.

**Caption:**
Real shearling is one piece of sheepskin or lambskin, tanned with the wool still attached to the hide.

It is not leather with wool sewn onto it. Not sherpa. Not shearling-look.

Three tests before you buy.

Pore test: real leather shows irregular hair follicles under a phone macro lens. Synthetic is too uniform, often patterned.

Backing test: bend the collar back. Real shearling shows smooth, slightly porous hide. Faux has woven fabric backing.

Fold test: real shearling has memory from the natural curve of the hide. Faux folds uniformly in any direction.

Ask the seller for a backing photo. If they can't provide one, you already have your answer.

## Post 08 — Authenticity Guarantee Limits
`topic_key: authenticity-guarantee-limits`
`#myth-busting #buyer-protection`

**TikTok Hook (first line):**
An authenticity guarantee does not mean the item is real. It means the platform has an opinion.

**Caption:**
Before you rely on any authentication badge, read what it actually covers.

Most platform guarantees exclude vintage items pre-2000. They exclude items sold by individual sellers in peer-to-peer transactions. They exclude items without original hang tags. They often have a minimum price threshold.

If a claim does get approved, some platforms require you to obtain a third-party authentication letter at your own expense first.

The most reliable protection is still checking what the item actually sells for on three other platforms before you buy. A listing priced 40 percent below the sold-price floor across every marketplace is flagging itself.

A badge tells you the platform's opinion. Sold comps tell you what the market knows.

#authenticating #luxuryresale #buyingvintage #resaletips #buyerprotection #vintagefashion
