6 ways to spot a fake designer item in 2026
```

**TikTok Description**
```
Super-fakes in 2026 are nearly undetectable to the naked eye. The buyers who don't get burned aren't luckier — they check 6 specific things every time.

Check 1: Logo fonts and stitching. Real luxury logos have perfect spacing and weight. Fakes print with wrong kerning or bunched embroidery at curves.
Check 2: Material feel. Authentic goods feel heavy and substantial. Fakes feel hollow or plasticky.
Check 3: Authenticity cards. Each card should have a unique serial that matches the interior tag. Non-sequential or repeated numbers = fake.
Check 4: Hardware. Real zippers glide smoothly and feel heavy. Fakes scratch or catch mid-zip.
Check 5: Brand-specific details. LV canvas never prints the fleur-de-lis on a seam. Gucci stripes are exactly 1cm each. Learn the tells for whatever you're buying.
Check 6: Closeup photos. If a seller can't provide closeups of tags, hardware, and serial numbers on request, walk away without exception.
