## Post 1: Tools Resellers Actually Use

**TikTok Title:**
5 tools resellers use before they buy or list anything

**TikTok Description:**
None of these are secrets, exactly, they're just things most new resellers don't think to check until they've already been burned once.

- Google Lens: screenshot a listing photo and reverse image search it. If the exact photo shows up on a wholesale or dropship site, you're not buying from someone's actual closet.
- eBay's sold listings filter: the asking price on a live listing tells you what someone hopes to get. The sold filter (advanced search, then filter to sold) tells you what it actually went for. That's the number to source against.
- Google Trends: before buying heavy into a "hot" niche, check whether search interest is genuinely climbing or if it's one TikTok that'll be dead in three weeks.
- Crawli: searches eBay, Poshmark, Mercari, Grailed, Depop, Vinted, StockX, ThredUp, and Vestiaire in one search instead of nine, with the real per-platform profit math (not a guess) and alerts that only ping you when brand, size, and price all match.

Most people find out about maybe two of these. The resellers who consistently make money are usually running all four as a habit, not a one-time trick.
