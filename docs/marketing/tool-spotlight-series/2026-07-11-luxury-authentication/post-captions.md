# Tool Spotlight — Post 2 (Luxury Authentication)

## Post 2: Never Get Scammed On A Designer Piece

**TikTok Title:**
5 tools so you never get scammed buying a designer piece

**TikTok Description:**
A fake and a real one look the same in a listing photo. The people who buy pre-owned luxury without getting burned just run a couple of these before they pay.

- The hardware and date code check: every house has a tell. Louis Vuitton moved to a microchip in 2021, Chanel has a hologram sticker, Hermès has a blind stamp. Learn your brand’s one marker and check it against the seller’s close ups.
- PurseForum’s “Authenticate This” threads: free. Post the seller’s photos in the brand’s thread and volunteers who authenticate all day will call it real or fake, usually within hours.
- A paid photo authentication: for anything in the four figures, $15 to $30 to a service like LegitGrails or Real Authentication is cheap insurance. Photo based, back in a day, with a certificate that holds up when you resell.
- Platforms that authenticate before it ships: Vestiaire’s Physical Check, The RealReal, Fashionphile, StockX. The item gets inspected by them before it reaches you.
- Crawli: the same bag sits on Vestiaire, Grailed, and StockX at three different prices. It searches all of them at once so you compare the authenticated options side by side instead of opening nine tabs.

Most people learn one of these the expensive way. The buyers who never get stuck with a fake are just running two or three of them as a habit.
